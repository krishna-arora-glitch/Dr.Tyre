Why It Matters

Raw lap time combines fuel burn, track evolution, and tyre degradation. The Ghost Baseline strips away the confounders, revealing the tyre-specific contribution to pace.

Observed vs Ghost Baseline (MEDIUM) OBSERVED

Ghost Baseline = Model-estimated counterfactual pace under controlled conditions. This is NOT physical rubber wear — it is the estimated tyre contribution to lap time.

The gap between the white (observed) and cyan (ghost baseline) lines is the estimated tyre-induced pace loss. Positive gap = tyre losing performance.
